The story

I'm buying back my freedom. You're invited.

I'm Brian — 48, corporate exec, Charleston, SC.

I put two daughters through college debt-free and kept nothing for myself. No savings, no safety net. I'm one of you.

So I'm buying back my freedom: $2,500 a month in recurring income by August 2027 — then I'm going nomad. The first thing that's just for me.

This site is home base for the journey: the videos, the numbers, and the small businesses I'm building along the way. No hype. No guru stuff. Just the road.
